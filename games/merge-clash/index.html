Your towers. Your next great strategy.

Merge Clash is a multiplayer tower defense game where every merge can change the battle. Build a five-tower team, defend against waves of enemies and play PvP or cooperative battles.

Build a team. Adapt to the next wave.

Bring a five-tower deck into a battle where each merge can change your options. Merge Clash combines tower collection and upgrades with the pressure of live tower defense.

Choose from more than 50 towers and explore PvP, co-op and Draft play. Boss abilities and changing merge outcomes give you a reason to keep adapting.

A few things you might wonder.

Can I play competitively or cooperate?

Yes. Play PvP battles, defend together in co-op or try Draft mode.

How many towers go into a deck?

Choose five towers with different abilities for your deck.

Does a merge keep the same tower type?

The resulting tower type can change at random, so every merge can alter the team on your board.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

On Android, Merge Clash contains ads and offers in-app purchases, including random items. See your store for current offers.

Where can I find purchase and privacy details?

See the offer in your store for prices, subscriptions and renewal terms. You can read our privacy policy before downloading.
