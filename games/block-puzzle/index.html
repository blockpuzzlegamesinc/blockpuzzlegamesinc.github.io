Make a little room for a better move.

Block Puzzle turns a small placement into a satisfying choice. Build lines, clear space and watch your score grow as the board changes.

On Android, play on an eight-by-eight grid with sets of three blocks, multiple-line bonuses and a saved high score. Its offline mode lets you keep a puzzle session within reach without Wi-Fi.
