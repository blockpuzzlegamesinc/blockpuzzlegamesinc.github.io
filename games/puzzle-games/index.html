Before you play.

Is Puzzle Games free?

Yes. Puzzle Games is a free download on the App Store. In-app purchases may be available; check the store listing for current options.

How do you play Puzzle Games?

Tap blocks to crush them. Collect blocks to complete level goals. Use power-ups on challenging boards.

What devices can I play Puzzle Games on?

Puzzle Games runs on iPhone and iPad. Apple devices need iOS 13.0 or later. The store provides the latest requirements and regional availability.

Who publishes this game?

The App Store lists Block Puzzle Games Inc. as the publisher of Puzzle Games. It was first released on 28 April 2018.

Where can I find privacy or purchase information?

Read our privacy policy and check the store listing for current purchase options, age rating and subscription terms. For help, email contact@puzzlecats.com.
