What kind of app is it?

Ball Games⋆ combines brick shooting with upgrade progression. Complete waves to earn in-game money, then choose upgrades that add balls or increase their speed and strength. The goal described in the iOS listing is to clear as many lines as possible.

This is a ball-shooting arcade and idle-style game, not a color-sorting puzzle or a sliding-ball maze. The public version string is v1.1 and its listed update dates from 2019. The store remains the place to check installation compatibility on your device; its presence does not establish a recent maintenance schedule.

Before you download.

Can I earn real money?

The description refers to in-game money used for upgrades. It does not establish real-world cash rewards or withdrawals.

Does idle mean progress continues when the app is closed?

The listing calls it an idle game, but offline or closed-app earnings were not verified. Do not rely on that label as a promise of background income.

Is this a ball-sort puzzle?

No. The verified gameplay is shooting balls into bricks and upgrading them, not sorting colored balls between containers.
