What kind of app is it?

Sudoku is a number-placement puzzle: fill a nine-by-nine grid so that each row, column and three-by-three box contains the digits one through nine without repetition. The numbers act as symbols, so progress comes from eliminating possibilities rather than performing arithmetic.

Sudoku· Classic Puzzle Games lists Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert puzzles, daily challenges, hints, notes, automatic checking, statistics and offline play. Those tools offer different ways to approach a session: keep candidate notes as you reason, use assistance when you want it, or select a difficulty that fits the time and attention you have available.

Difficulty labels are not standardized across Sudoku apps. Likewise, a hint button alone does not tell you whether a puzzle will receive a detailed explanation of the next logical technique. The verified facts on this page do not establish the depth of every hint, a particular rating method, or current availability of every variant mentioned in store marketing.

Do I need to be good at mathematics?

Classic Sudoku uses the digits as distinct symbols. Its central task is placing each symbol without repeating it in a row, column or box; arithmetic is not required.

Can I use notes and play offline?

Yes. The public iOS and Android descriptions advertise candidate notes and offline play. Offline gameplay is not a statement that the app never makes network requests when connected.
