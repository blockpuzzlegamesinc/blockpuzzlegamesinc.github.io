What kind of app is it?

Coloring Games· is a pixel-coloring app, rather than a freehand drawing canvas. The iOS listing describes tapping individual squares or holding and swiping across several squares, with a collection of pictures to complete.

You can also create a coloring project from a camera picture. The listing describes a bomb tool for a large area and a wand that fills an entire number. Tool costs, camera-photo processing and storage practices are not established here; check the current app disclosures before using a personal picture.

Before you download.

Is this freehand drawing?

The verified listing describes coloring pixel-art squares and numbered areas. It does not establish a general-purpose freehand drawing tool.

Can I color a photo?

The iOS description says you can take a camera picture and color it. It does not establish whether photos are processed only on the device or how they are stored.

The bomb and wand are listed, but current limits and costs were not verified. A free initial download does not mean every tool or offer is included.
