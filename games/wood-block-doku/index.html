Before you play.

Is Wood Block Doku: Brain Games free?

Yes. Wood Block Doku: Brain Games is a free download on the App Store and Google Play. In-app purchases may be available; check the store listing for current options.

How do you play Wood Block Doku: Brain Games?

Clear full lines and 3 × 3 squares. Play in portrait or landscape. Continue offline without Wi-Fi.

What devices can I play Wood Block Doku: Brain Games on?

Wood Block Doku: Brain Games runs on iPhone, iPad and Android. Apple devices need iOS 15.0 or later. You can also play Wood Block Doku: Brain Games in your browser. The store provides the latest requirements and regional availability.

Who publishes this game?

The App Store lists Block Puzzle Games Inc. as the publisher of Wood Block Doku: Brain Games. It was first released on 20 November 2022.
