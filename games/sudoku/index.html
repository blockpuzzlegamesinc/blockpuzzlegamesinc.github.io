Is Sudoku Classic Puzzle Games free?

Yes. Sudoku Classic Puzzle Games is a free download on the App Store and Google Play. In-app purchases may be available; check the store listing for current options.

How do you play Sudoku Classic Puzzle Games?

Choose Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert. Return for daily Sudoku challenges. Use hints and play offline.

What devices can I play Sudoku Classic Puzzle Games on?

Sudoku Classic Puzzle Games runs on iPhone, iPad and Android. Apple devices need iOS 15.0 or later. You can also play Sudoku Classic Puzzle Games in your browser. The store provides the latest requirements and regional availability.

Who publishes this game?
