A few things you might wonder.

Can I choose the difficulty?

Yes. Choose Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert, or return for a daily challenge.

Can I use notes and hints?

Yes. Candidate notes, hints and automatic checking are available.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Offline play is available on iOS and Android.

Is Premium available?

On iPhone and iPad, optional monthly and annual Premium subscriptions are available in supported regions. The in-app offer shows the current price and renewal terms.

Where can I find purchase and privacy details?

See the offer in your store for prices, subscriptions and renewal terms. You can read our privacy policy before downloading.
