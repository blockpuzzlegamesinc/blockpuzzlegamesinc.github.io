What kind of app is it?

Block Puzzle turns a simple placement decision into a space-management challenge. Place shapes into open cells, complete lines to clear space and keep enough room for the next pieces. The iOS listing presents this core loop as a high-score game: each placement affects both the points available now and the spaces you will need later.

The Android description is more specific about its rules. It describes an eight-by-eight grid, sets of three blocks, bonuses for clearing multiple lines and a saved high score. A round ends when the remaining pieces cannot be placed. That makes the shape of your empty space an important part of planning, alongside the number of empty cells.

A frustrating sequence of pieces is not evidence that a game changes its rules to stop a high score. The public descriptions do not give us a reproducible specification for the piece generator. We describe the visible placement game without claiming a particular randomness algorithm, guaranteed solvability or a fairness result we have not measured.

Before you download.

Is this the same as a falling-block game?

The listed core mechanic is placing supplied shapes into a grid and clearing completed lines. It should not be confused with a game where pieces continuously fall under a timer.

Does the app record detailed performance statistics?

The Android listing describes a saved high score. We have not verified detailed session history, piece-generation analysis or a broader statistics suite.

Is it ad-free or free of in-app purchases?

We cannot promise either. The Google Play purchase label conflicts with wording in its description; check the current store information and in-game offer before making a purchase decision.
