Before you play.

Is Block Out: Unblock Tile free?

Yes. Block Out: Unblock Tile is a free download on the App Store. In-app purchases may be available; check the store listing for current options.

How do you play Block Out: Unblock Tile?

Slide blocks to unblock the red brick. Choose among several difficulty levels. Use hints and undo, online or offline.

What devices can I play Block Out: Unblock Tile on?

Block Out: Unblock Tile runs on iPhone and iPad. Apple devices need iOS 13.0 or later. The store provides the latest requirements and regional availability.
