Support articles

Wrong numbers?

Wrong numbers are denoted in red on the grid. Mistakes are tracked in the game when an incorrect number is entered. If you wish to play without automatic mistake check Go to Settings and turn on Manually Checking Mistakes

Turn off small pencil numbers?

If you wish to play without automatic pencils, In Settings, toggle off Autofill Pencil

Want larger numbers or a new design?

Tap the painter’s palette icon to change font size and theme Toggle Use Classic UI for the old look

Found a bug?

Tap the mail icon above to send us the bug report, please do not alter any of the pre-populated data

Turn off sound effects?

Go to Settings > toggle off Sound

How do I remove ads?

Go to Settings > Turn off advertisements. Pick monthly or annual Premium

Still see ads after purchase?

Go to Settings > Turn off advertisements > Restore subscription. Check your device’s subscription settings

How do I report an advertisement?

To report an advertisement, please take a screenshot and contact our support team with details about the ad and when it appeared.

Didn’t get a purchase?

Email us via Settings > Help > Contact Us with your receipt

How to Cancel a Subscription

On iPhone or iPad (iOS): Open the Settings app Tap your name at the top Tap Subscriptions Choose the subscription you want to cancel Tap Cancel Subscription On Android (Google Play): Open the Play Store app Tap your profile picture (top right) Tap Payments & subscriptions Tap Subscriptions Choose the subscription you want to cancel Tap Cancel subscription

Can I restore data to a current or new device?

For Solitaire, restoring data is possible by logging into the same Apple or Google Play account used for your purchase. Game progress may not transfer if it is not linked to an account. Please contact support for help with specific cases.

App crashes on launch (iOS 17)

We are aware of an issue causing crashes on iOS 17. Please update to the latest version of the app. If the problem persists, contact support.
