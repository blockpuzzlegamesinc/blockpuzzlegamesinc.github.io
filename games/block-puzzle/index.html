Before you play.

Is Block Puzzle free?

Yes. Block Puzzle is a free download on the App Store and Google Play. In-app purchases may be available; check the store listing for current options.

How do you play Block Puzzle?

Place each block on the grid. Complete lines to clear them. Try new arrangements and challenge your high score.

What devices can I play Block Puzzle on?

Block Puzzle runs on iPhone, iPad and Android. Apple devices need iOS 15.0 or later. You can also play Block Puzzle in your browser. The store provides the latest requirements and regional availability.

Who publishes this game?

The App Store lists Block Puzzle Games Inc. as the publisher of Block Puzzle. It was first released on 13 March 2018.

Where can I find privacy or purchase information?
