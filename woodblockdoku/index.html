Wood Block Doku - Wood Block Puzzle

How to Play Wood Block Doku

Play Wood Block Doku, the addictive wood block puzzle game that combines Tetris and Sudoku. Place wooden blocks strategically to clear lines and columns.

Game Features:

  • Free to play online - no download required
  • Combines block puzzle and Sudoku mechanics
  • Strategic play with no time pressure
  • Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile
  • Beautiful wooden block graphics

How to Play:

  1. Drag and drop blocks onto the game board
  2. Create complete horizontal or vertical lines to clear them
  3. Clear multiple lines at once for bonus points
  4. Game ends when you can't place any more blocks
  5. Try to beat your high score!

Wood Block Doku combines block-puzzle placement with a 9x9 Sudoku-style grid.
