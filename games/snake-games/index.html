What kind of app is it?

Snake Games: Arcade keeps the familiar idea easy to understand: collect stars to grow, then steer clear of your own body. Its block-by-block movement gives every turn a consequence as the snake gets longer.

The current iPhone and iPad listing describes retro controls and unlimited lives. Version 1.2 release notes add more themes and updated gameplay. Pick it for a compact arcade challenge rather than assuming it includes multiplayer, an online leaderboard or an offline mode that the listing has not established.
